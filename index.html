Privacy Policy

Smoke Free Quest (Android app, package com.smokefreequest.game)
Effective date: September 26, 2026

The short version: Smoke Free Quest does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

1. Who we are

Smoke Free Quest is developed by Wasim ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at helixhank799@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter in the app

To track your progress, the app asks for a few details. They are used only inside the app on your device:

InformationWhy it is used
Hero name (any nickname)Shown in the game
Cigarettes per day, cigarettes per pack, pack price and currencyTo calculate money saved, cigarettes avoided and in-game loot
Your quit date and timeTo run your smoke-free timer and unlock bosses
Your reasons for quitting (optional)Shown back to you as motivation
Game progress (level, gold, gear, achievements, logged slips, cravings beaten)To save your game

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID, health data from other apps, or any usage analytics. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

4. Device permissions

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsLocal reminders when a boss appears or loot chests are waiting. You can turn these off in the app or in Android settings.
VibrationHaptic feedback during the game (can be turned off in the app).
Run at startupRestores your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.

Notifications are created on your device; they are not sent from a server.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any data. If you tap "Share my progress", the app opens Android's standard share menu with a short text message, and you choose whether and where to send it.

6. Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's saved game in your personal device backup (for example, to your Google account), so your progress can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and your backup provider, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can manage it in your phone's backup settings.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time with Me → Erase save data in the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is sent over the network, there is no data in transit to protect.

9. Children

Smoke Free Quest is designed for adults who want to quit smoking and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Health disclaimer

The app is a motivational game and does not provide medical advice. Health milestones shown in the app are general estimates. For help quitting, talk to a doctor or a local quitline.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy before that version is released.
